The model's original object names are retained in the scene; system labels in the app are convenience groupings and are not assertions of factory CAD nomenclature.

The model is a community asset published under the title above. AeroLab 25 does not claim that it is official Red Bull Racing CAD, a scan, or dimensionally certified. Its exact configuration and geometry require comparison with independent 2025 RB21 references.
